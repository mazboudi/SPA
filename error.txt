Elements of this architecture were possible previously, but implementing them globally would have required substantial custom integration, endpoint software deployment, broad VPN access, and operational compromises. Today, mature browser-based host access, enterprise IAM and MFA, zero-trust private application access, and centralized telemetry make it possible to remove direct endpoint-to-mainframe connectivity without sacrificing the user experience or operational scale required by the business.

The executive argument

The organization has historically protected its most critical mainframe systems using an access model created when users, devices, and networks were largely internal and trusted. That model is no longer aligned with the current threat landscape or the distributed nature of the workforce and client base. The proposed solution removes direct external pathways, centralizes control, enforces identity-based least privilege, limits the blast radius of compromised endpoints and credentials, and creates auditable visibility over every access session. The reason to act now is that the cyber risk has materially increased while the technology required to implement a practical replacement has matured.
